# Chocolate Fondant

> Individual molten chocolate cakes with a liquid centre — the dinner-party showstopper that can be made ahead and baked to order in 12 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 150 g (5.3 oz) 70% dark chocolate, chopped
- 150 g (⅔ cup) unsalted butter, plus extra for greasing
- 4 large eggs + 4 large egg yolks
- 150 g (¾ cup) caster sugar
- 70 g (½ cup) plain (all-purpose) flour, sifted
- Cocoa powder, for dusting moulds
- Pinch of fine sea salt
- Vanilla ice cream or crème fraîche, to serve

## Instructions

1. **Prep moulds:** Butter 4 × 180 ml (6 oz) ramekins generously. Dust with cocoa powder, tapping out the excess. Refrigerate.
2. **Melt chocolate:** Melt butter and chocolate together in a heatproof bowl over a pan of barely simmering water (bain-marie), stirring gently. Do not let the bowl touch the water. Remove from heat and let cool 5 minutes.
3. **Whisk eggs:** Whisk whole eggs, egg yolks, and sugar together with electric beaters until pale, thick, and tripled in volume — about 4–5 minutes.
4. **Combine:** Fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture gently using a large spatula. Add sifted flour and salt; fold until just combined. Do not overmix.
5. **Fill moulds:** Divide batter evenly among the prepared ramekins. At this point you can refrigerate for up to 24 hours.
6. **Bake:** Preheat oven to 200 °C / 390 °F. Bake straight from the fridge for 12–13 minutes (or 10–11 minutes if at room temperature). The edges should be set; the centre should still have a slight wobble.
7. **Turn out:** Run a knife around the edge, place a warm plate on top, and invert confidently. Serve immediately with ice cream.

## Chef's Tips

- The 24-hour fridge rest actually improves the texture — the batter firms up for a cleaner molten centre.
- Test one fondant first if it's your first time — oven temperatures vary. Adjust by 1 minute.
- Use good-quality 70% chocolate; it makes a significant difference.
- A pinch of espresso powder in the batter deepens the chocolate flavour without adding coffee taste.
