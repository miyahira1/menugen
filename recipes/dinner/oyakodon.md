# Oyakodon

> "Parent and child" rice bowl — tender chicken and softly set egg simmered in a sweet dashi broth, ladled over steamed rice. A beloved Japanese comfort classic.

## Ingredients

- 600 g (1.3 lbs) boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 6 large eggs
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 4 portions steamed Japanese short-grain rice

**Simmering broth:**
- 240 ml (1 cup) dashi stock
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp sake
- 1 tbsp sugar

**To garnish:**
- Mitsuba leaves or spring onion, thinly sliced
- Shichimi togarashi (Japanese 7-spice), optional

## Instructions

1. **Mix broth:** Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar in a bowl. Stir to dissolve sugar.
2. **Cook in batches (serves 2 per batch):** Use a small skillet (about 22 cm / 9 in) — this dish is best made in 2 portions for cleaner results.
3. **Simmer onion:** Pour half the broth into the skillet. Add half the onion. Simmer over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until onion softens.
4. **Add chicken:** Add half the chicken pieces in a single layer. Cook 4 minutes, turning once, until just cooked through.
5. **Add eggs:** Beat 3 eggs lightly (they should have swirls of yolk visible, not uniform). Pour in a circular motion over the chicken. Cover and cook on medium-low for 1 minute 30 seconds — the egg should be half-set, still slightly runny and silky. Remove from heat; residual heat finishes it.
6. **Serve:** Slide the entire contents over a bowl of steamed rice. Garnish with spring onion.
7. **Repeat** for the second batch.

## Chef's Tips

- The barely-set egg is the hallmark — err on the side of undercooking; it sets further on the hot rice.
- Beat eggs gently; you want streaks of yolk and white visible in the finished dish.
- Add a handful of shiitake mushrooms with the onion for extra depth.
- If making all 4 portions at once, use a 30 cm skillet and work quickly.
