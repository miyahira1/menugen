# Yakitori

> Japanese grilled chicken skewers with a smoky, sweet tare glaze — the definitive izakaya food that works perfectly on a home grill or griddle pan.

## Ingredients

**Skewers:**
- 600 g (1.3 lbs) boneless chicken thighs, skin-on, cut into 3 cm cubes
- 200 g (7 oz) chicken livers, trimmed (optional, for variety)
- 4 spring onions, cut into 4 cm lengths
- 12–16 bamboo skewers, soaked in water 30 mins

**Tare (glaze):**
- 100 ml (⅓ cup + 1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 60 ml (¼ cup) mirin
- 2 tbsp sake
- 2 tbsp sugar

**To serve:**
- Shichimi togarashi (Japanese 7-spice)
- Cold Sapporo or Japanese beer

## Instructions

1. **Make tare:** Combine soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer for 8–10 minutes until slightly syrupy. It will thicken more as it cools. Set aside.
2. **Skewer:** Thread 3–4 cubes of chicken onto each skewer, alternating with spring onion pieces. If using livers, skewer them separately as they cook faster.
3. **First cook:** Grill over high heat (charcoal is ideal; cast-iron grill pan works well) for 3–4 minutes per side. The chicken should be almost cooked through.
4. **Glaze:** Brush generously with tare. Return to heat for 1 minute, brush again, and repeat twice more — building up layers of glaze.
5. **Serve immediately** with shichimi togarashi on the side for dusting.

## Chef's Tips

- Charcoal gives yakitori its distinctive smoky flavour — even one round of charcoal grilling beats a gas grill.
- "Negima" style (chicken + spring onion) is the most popular combination; try also chicken skin skewers (kawa) on their own — render the skin slowly until crispy.
- The tare keeps indefinitely in the fridge; some restaurants add to their tare sauce for decades, never washing the pot.
- A sprinkle of salt (shio) instead of tare on chicken thighs is equally delicious and lets the meat shine.
