# Salmon Teriyaki

> Pan-seared salmon fillets lacquered with a classic sweet-savoury teriyaki glaze — ready in under 20 minutes and endlessly satisfying over a bowl of steamed rice.

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets, skin-on (about 160–180 g / 6 oz each)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Salt and white pepper

**Teriyaki glaze:**
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp sake
- 1 tbsp sugar

**To serve:**
- Steamed Japanese rice
- Steamed or blanched broccolini or pak choi
- Sesame seeds and spring onion, to garnish
- Pickled ginger, optional

## Instructions

1. **Mix glaze:** Stir soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar together until sugar dissolves. Set aside.
2. **Prep salmon:** Pat salmon fillets completely dry. Season lightly with salt and white pepper.
3. **Sear skin side:** Heat oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place salmon skin-side down. Press gently with a spatula for the first 30 seconds to prevent curling. Cook for 4–5 minutes until skin is golden and crisp. The salmon should be cooked about ¾ of the way through.
4. **Flip:** Turn salmon flesh-side down. Cook for 1 minute.
5. **Glaze:** Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour teriyaki glaze into the pan. Spoon glaze over salmon repeatedly as it reduces and thickens — about 2 minutes. Watch carefully; sugar burns quickly.
6. **Serve:** Place salmon on rice, spoon remaining glaze over the top. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion. Serve with steamed greens.

## Chef's Tips

- Starting with a dry fillet is critical for crispy skin — moisture causes steaming instead of searing.
- Medium-to-medium-high heat lets the fat render from the skin without burning; patience here gives a satisfying crunch.
- The glaze can be made in a larger batch and stored in the fridge for 2 weeks — it works on chicken, tofu, and aubergine too.
- For a slightly richer flavour, add ½ tsp grated ginger to the glaze.
