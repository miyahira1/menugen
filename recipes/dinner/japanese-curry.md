# Japanese Curry

> Japan's most popular home-cooked dish — thick, mild, and deeply sweet-savoury. Made with curry roux blocks, it's one of the most comforting and forgiving recipes in Japanese cooking.

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lbs) beef chuck or stew beef, cut into 3 cm cubes
- 3 medium potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks
- 2 carrots, cut into rolling diagonal cuts
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 100 g (3.5 oz) Japanese curry roux blocks (e.g. S&B Golden Curry, medium hot)
- 800 ml (3⅓ cups) water or beef stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp ketchup (the Japanese "secret" ingredient)
- 4 portions steamed Japanese rice
- Fukujinzuke (pickled vegetables), to serve

## Instructions

1. **Brown beef:** Heat oil in a large heavy pot over high heat. Season beef with salt and pepper. Brown in batches on all sides, about 4 minutes total per batch. Set aside.
2. **Soften onion:** In the same pot, cook onion over medium heat for 8 minutes until soft and turning golden. Add carrots and stir for 2 minutes.
3. **Build:** Return beef to pot. Pour in water or stock. Bring to a boil, skimming foam. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 20 minutes.
4. **Add potatoes:** Add potato chunks. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes until potatoes are tender and easily pierced with a knife.
5. **Add roux:** Break curry roux into segments and add to the pot. Stir gently until fully dissolved and the sauce thickens — about 5 minutes over low heat.
6. **Season:** Stir in soy sauce and ketchup. Simmer gently for 5 more minutes. If too thick, add a splash of water.
7. **Serve** ladled over rice with fukujinzuke on the side.

## Chef's Tips

- Using two different brands/heat levels of roux creates more complex flavour.
- A grated apple or 1 tbsp of mango chutney stirred in adds a traditional sweetness.
- Japanese curry freezes beautifully — freeze without potatoes (they turn grainy) and add freshly cooked ones when reheating.
- The ketchup and soy sauce are authentic additions used in Japanese households; don't skip them.
