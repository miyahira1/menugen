# Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken

> A classic French-style roast chicken with crispy golden skin, perfumed with lemon and garlic — simple enough for a weeknight, impressive enough for guests.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, ~1.8 kg (4 lbs)
- 1 whole head of garlic, halved crosswise
- 2 lemons — 1 zested + juiced, 1 halved
- 60 g (4 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp fine sea salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tsp dried thyme (or 4 fresh sprigs)
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 250 ml (1 cup) dry white wine or chicken stock

## Instructions

1. **Preheat** oven to 220 °C / 425 °F (fan 200 °C). Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking.
2. **Make the herb butter:** Mix softened butter with lemon zest, half the thyme, half the rosemary, ½ tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper.
3. **Prepare the chicken:** Pat dry with paper towels. Gently loosen the skin over the breast and thighs with your fingers, then push the herb butter underneath the skin, spreading evenly.
4. **Season:** Rub the outside of the chicken with olive oil and the remaining salt, pepper, thyme, and rosemary.
5. **Stuff the cavity** with the lemon halves and garlic head halves.
6. **Roast:** Place chicken breast-side up in a roasting tin. Pour wine or stock around (not over) the chicken. Roast at 220 °C for 20 minutes to brown the skin.
7. **Reduce heat** to 190 °C / 375 °F and roast for a further 60–70 minutes, basting every 20 minutes, until juices run clear when you pierce the thigh and internal temp reaches 75 °C / 165 °F.
8. **Rest** for 15 minutes loosely tented with foil before carving.
9. **Make a quick pan sauce:** Squeeze the roasted lemon from the cavity into the pan juices, discard fat if needed, and bring to a simmer over the hob. Season to taste.

## Chef's Tips

- Dry-brining the chicken (salt it uncovered in the fridge overnight) gives dramatically crispier skin.
- Adding 4 halved shallots and 2 carrots to the roasting tin creates a built-in side.
- The roasted garlic cloves spread like butter — serve alongside.
- Leftover chicken is excellent in sandwiches with the pan sauce as a condiment.
