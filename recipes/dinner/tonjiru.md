# Tonjiru

> Japan's heartiest miso soup — loaded with pork belly, burdock root, daikon, carrot, and konnyaku in a rich miso broth. A warming meal-in-a-bowl for cold nights.

## Ingredients

- 200 g (7 oz) pork belly, thinly sliced, cut into 3 cm strips
- 150 g (5 oz) daikon radish, peeled and cut into half-moons
- 150 g (5 oz) carrot, peeled and cut into half-moons
- 100 g (3.5 oz) burdock root (gobo), scrubbed and sliced diagonally — soak in water 10 mins
- 150 g (5 oz) konnyaku (konjac), torn into bite-sized pieces
- 150 g (5 oz) firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1.2 litres (5 cups) dashi stock
- 5 tbsp mixed miso paste (3 tbsp white + 2 tbsp red)
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Shichimi togarashi, to serve

## Instructions

1. **Sauté pork:** Heat sesame oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add pork slices and cook for 2–3 minutes until lightly browned. Don't drain the fat — it forms the base of the broth.
2. **Add root vegetables:** Add burdock, carrot, and daikon. Stir-fry with the pork for 3 minutes. Add konnyaku and stir for 1 more minute.
3. **Add stock:** Pour in dashi. Bring to a boil, skimming any foam. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes until root vegetables are just tender.
4. **Add tofu:** Add tofu cubes and simmer 3 more minutes.
5. **Dissolve miso:** Reduce heat to low. Place miso paste in a ladle or small strainer, lower into the broth, and stir until dissolved. Add soy sauce. Do not boil again.
6. **Serve:** Ladle into deep bowls. Garnish with spring onion and sesame seeds. Offer shichimi togarashi on the side.

## Chef's Tips

- Burdock root (gobo) has an earthy, artichoke-like flavour that is key to authentic tonjiru — worth finding at Asian grocery stores.
- Konnyaku has a pleasantly chewy, gelatinous texture — tearing rather than cutting it gives it more surface area to absorb flavour.
- This soup thickens as it sits; add a splash of dashi when reheating.
- Tonjiru is traditionally eaten in winter — the fat in the pork belly makes it deeply warming.
