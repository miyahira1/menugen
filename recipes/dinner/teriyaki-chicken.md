# Teriyaki Chicken

> Glossy, sweet-savoury glazed chicken thighs that caramelise beautifully in a hot pan — on the table in 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 8 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1 kg / 2.2 lbs)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

**Teriyaki sauce:**
- 80 ml (⅓ cup) soy sauce
- 80 ml (⅓ cup) mirin
- 2 tbsp sake (or dry sherry)
- 2 tbsp sugar

**To serve:**
- Steamed Japanese rice
- Shredded cabbage or cucumber ribbons
- Sesame seeds and spring onion, to garnish

## Instructions

1. **Mix sauce:** Whisk soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar together in a small bowl until sugar dissolves. Set aside.
2. **Prep chicken:** Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Score the thickest parts of the flesh 2–3 times with a knife so they cook evenly.
3. **Sear:** Heat oil in a large non-stick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Place chicken skin-side down. Cook without moving for 6–7 minutes until skin is deeply golden and crisp. Flip and cook the other side for 4 minutes.
4. **Glaze:** Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in the teriyaki sauce. Spoon sauce over chicken repeatedly as it reduces and thickens — about 3 minutes. The sauce should coat the chicken in a shiny glaze. Watch carefully; the sugar burns easily.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest for 3 minutes. Slice and serve over rice with shredded cabbage. Spoon remaining glaze over the top. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion.

## Chef's Tips

- Skin-on thighs give the best result — the caramelised skin is the star.
- The sauce keeps in the fridge for 2 weeks; double the batch and use on salmon, tofu, or aubergine.
- For a baked version: sear briefly, then bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes, glazing twice.
- A splash of sake in the pan while searing adds steaming that speeds cooking through thicker parts.
