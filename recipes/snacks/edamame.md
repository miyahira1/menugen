# Edamame with Sea Salt

> The simplest and most satisfying Japanese snack — young soybeans boiled and generously salted. The definitive beer companion at every izakaya in Japan.

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lbs) frozen edamame in pods
- 2 tbsp fine sea salt (for cooking water)
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt (Maldon or similar), for finishing
- Optional: ½ tsp togarashi powder or sesame oil drizzle for a variation

## Instructions

1. **Boil water:** Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Add 2 tbsp sea salt — the water should taste like the sea.
2. **Cook:** Add frozen edamame. Boil for 4–5 minutes until bright green and tender when you squeeze a pod.
3. **Drain:** Drain immediately. Do not rinse — you want the salt on the pods.
4. **Season:** Scatter flaky sea salt generously over the hot edamame in a bowl. Toss to coat.
5. **Serve** in a large communal bowl. Pop the beans directly into your mouth from the pod; discard the pod.

## Chef's Tips

- Taste as you go — edamame should be tender but have slight resistance, not mushy.
- Adding 1 tbsp of salt directly to the edamame and rubbing them together (momigara) before boiling removes fine hairs and helps the salt cling.
- For garlic edamame: toss in 1 tsp sesame oil and ½ tsp garlic powder while still hot.
- For spicy edamame: dust with shichimi togarashi and a drizzle of chilli oil.
- Serve hot or at room temperature — never cold.
