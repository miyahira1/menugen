# Temaki Hand Rolls

> Cone-shaped hand rolls you assemble yourself at the table — sushi rice, sashimi-grade fish, and fresh fillings rolled in a crisp sheet of nori. Interactive, casual, and endlessly fun.

## Ingredients

**Sushi rice:**
- 360 g (2 cups) Japanese short-grain rice
- 430 ml (1¾ cups) water
- 60 ml (¼ cup) rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp fine salt

**Fillings (choose a mix):**
- 300 g (10 oz) sashimi-grade salmon, sliced into strips
- 200 g (7 oz) sashimi-grade tuna, sliced
- 1 small cucumber, julienned
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- 4 tbsp tobiko (flying fish roe), optional
- Japanese mayo (Kewpie)

**Assembly:**
- 8 sheets nori, cut in half to make 16 half-sheets
- Soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger (gari), to serve

## Instructions

1. **Cook rice:** Rinse rice until water is clear. Add to pot with 430 ml water. Bring to boil, reduce to lowest heat, cover, and cook 12 minutes. Rest covered 10 minutes.
2. **Season rice:** Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl — stir until dissolved. Spread hot rice in a wide, flat container (a wooden hangiri or baking tray). Pour seasoned vinegar over, folding gently with a spatula while fanning to cool quickly. The rice should be glossy and sticky. Do not refrigerate.
3. **Prep fillings:** Slice fish, cucumber, and avocado. Arrange all fillings attractively on a large plate.
4. **Assemble at the table:** Each person takes a half-sheet of nori (shiny side down, in their palm), spoons 2–3 tbsp of sushi rice diagonally on one half, then adds filling strips. Roll into a cone shape and eat immediately (nori softens within 60 seconds).
5. **Dipping sauce:** A small dish of soy sauce with a dab of wasabi alongside. Do not mix wasabi into the soy sauce — dip the roll briefly.

## Chef's Tips

- Sushi rice is the most important element — the seasoning balance (vinegar, sweet, salt) is everything. Taste and adjust.
- Nori must be fresh and stored in an airtight bag — stale nori is chewy and unpleasant.
- Fan the rice while mixing the vinegar in — this creates the signature gloss.
- Serve all components at room temperature; cold fish lacks flavour and cold rice turns hard.
