# Almond Flour Pancakes (Baby)

> Soft, fluffy mini pancakes made with almond flour — naturally moist, rich in healthy fats and protein, with no added sugar or salt. The mashed banana sweetens them naturally.

> **Baby note:** This recipe makes 4 toddler portions. Contains eggs and tree nuts (almonds) — introduce cautiously if these are new foods for your baby. No added sugar, no salt, no honey. Cut into small finger-food pieces before serving.

## Ingredients

*(4 toddler portions — about 12–14 small pancakes)*

- 120 g (1¼ cups) almond flour (blanched, not almond meal)
- 1 very ripe banana, mashed (about 100 g)
- 2 large eggs
- 60 ml (¼ cup) whole milk, breast milk, or unsweetened oat milk
- ¼ tsp baking powder (aluminium-free)
- ¼ tsp vanilla extract (optional, alcohol-free if possible)
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp coconut oil or unsalted butter, for cooking

## Instructions

1. **Mash banana:** Mash the ripe banana in a large bowl until very smooth.
2. **Mix wet ingredients:** Add eggs, milk, and vanilla to the mashed banana. Whisk well to combine.
3. **Add dry ingredients:** Add almond flour, baking powder, and cinnamon. Stir until a smooth batter forms. Let rest 2 minutes — the batter will thicken slightly.
4. **Heat pan:** Place a non-stick skillet over low-medium heat. Add a tiny amount of coconut oil or butter.
5. **Cook small pancakes:** Drop 1 tbsp of batter per pancake (keep them small — about 6 cm). Cook for 2–3 minutes until the edges are set and the top looks mostly dry with a few bubbles.
6. **Flip gently:** Almond flour pancakes are more delicate than wheat ones. Use a thin spatula and flip carefully. Cook 1–2 minutes more until golden.
7. **Cool completely** on a plate before serving. Cut into strips or small bite-sized pieces.

## Chef's Tips

- Almond flour pancakes are naturally more moist and fragile than regular pancakes — low heat and patience are key.
- If the batter is too thick, add a splash more milk. If too thin, add a tablespoon more almond flour.
- These are naturally sweeter and richer than oat-based pancakes — a good option for a toddler who needs extra calories.
- Fold in 2 tbsp of finely grated apple or courgette (zucchini) for added nutrition — the baby won't notice.
- Freeze well: cool completely, freeze flat, then store in a bag for up to 1 month.
