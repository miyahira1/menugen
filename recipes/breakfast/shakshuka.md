# Shakshuka

> Eggs poached in a spiced, garlicky tomato sauce — a one-pan North African classic that works for breakfast, brunch, or a lazy dinner.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- 800 g (2 × 400 g tins) crushed or whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- ½ tsp sugar (balances acidity)
- 8 large eggs
- 100 g (3.5 oz) feta cheese, crumbled
- Small bunch flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Crusty bread, to serve

## Instructions

1. **Soften aromatics:** Heat olive oil in a large, wide skillet or sauté pan over medium heat. Add onion and pepper; cook 8 minutes until soft and lightly golden. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more.
2. **Bloom spices:** Add cumin, paprika, coriander, and cayenne. Stir and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. **Build the sauce:** Add tomatoes, salt, and sugar. Break up any large pieces. Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens slightly. Taste and adjust seasoning.
4. **Add eggs:** Using a spoon, create 8 small wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well. Season the eggs lightly with salt.
5. **Poach:** Cover the pan with a lid and cook for 5–8 minutes. Check at 5 minutes — whites should be just set but yolks still runny (6 min for medium, 8 min for firm).
6. **Serve:** Scatter feta and parsley over the top. Bring the pan straight to the table with crusty bread.

## Chef's Tips

- Adding a handful of baby spinach into the sauce before the eggs adds colour and nutrition.
- A harissa swirl in the sauce adds depth and heat.
- For a richer version, stir 1 tbsp tomato paste in with the spices.
- Leftovers reheat well — add a splash of water to loosen the sauce.
