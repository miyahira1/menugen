# 2-Ingredient Banana Pancakes (Baby)

> The simplest pancake in existence — just banana and egg, naturally sweet, soft enough for gums, and completely free of added sugar, salt, or honey. Perfect for baby-led weaning from 6 months+.

> **Baby note:** This recipe makes 4 toddler portions (about 3 small pancakes each). No added sugar, no salt, no honey. Suitable from 6 months+. Always supervise during eating and cut into small pieces appropriate for your child's stage.

## Ingredients

*(4 toddler portions — about 12 small pancakes)*

- 2 very ripe bananas (the riper the better — more natural sweetness)
- 2 large eggs
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
- 1 tsp coconut oil or unsalted butter, for cooking

## Instructions

1. **Mash bananas:** In a bowl, mash bananas with a fork until completely smooth — no large lumps. The riper the banana, the sweeter and easier to mash.
2. **Add eggs:** Crack eggs into the banana mixture. Stir vigorously until fully combined into a smooth batter. Add cinnamon if using.
3. **Heat pan:** Heat a non-stick skillet over low-medium heat. Add a very small amount of coconut oil or butter — just enough to coat.
4. **Cook small:** Drop 1 tablespoon of batter per pancake. These should be small (about 6 cm / 2.5 in) — easier for little hands and safer for toddlers. Cook for 2 minutes until the edges look set and the surface has a few bubbles.
5. **Flip carefully:** These pancakes are delicate. Flip gently and cook 1–2 minutes more until golden.
6. **Cool completely** before serving. Cut into small strips or pieces for your toddler.
7. **Serve** at room temperature — never hot.

## Chef's Tips

- The riper the banana (covered in dark spots), the better — overripe bananas mash smoother and are sweeter.
- These pancakes are fragile because there is no flour or binder — keep them small and the heat low.
- Add 1 tbsp of peanut butter (if no nut allergy) to the batter for protein and healthy fats.
- Freeze cooled pancakes in a single layer, then bag — reheat in a dry pan or toaster for a quick breakfast.
- For older toddlers: top with a thin spread of unsweetened nut butter or a few fresh blueberries.
