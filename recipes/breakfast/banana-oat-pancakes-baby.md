# Banana Oat Pancakes (Baby)

> Hearty, naturally sweet pancakes made from blended oats and ripe banana — high in fibre, iron, and slow-release energy. A filling breakfast for busy toddlers, with no added sugar or salt.

> **Baby note:** This recipe makes 4 toddler portions. Oats contain avenin (a protein similar to gluten) — if your baby has a gluten sensitivity, use certified gluten-free oats. No added sugar, no salt, no honey. Suitable from 6 months+.

## Ingredients

*(4 toddler portions — about 14–16 small pancakes)*

- 100 g (1 cup) rolled oats — **or instant oats** (see Chef's Tips)
- 2 very ripe bananas (about 200 g mashed)
- 2 large eggs
- 80 ml (⅓ cup) whole milk, breast milk, or unsweetened oat milk
- ¼ tsp baking powder (aluminium-free)
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- 1 tsp coconut oil or unsalted butter, for cooking

**Optional mix-ins (for variety):**
- 1 tbsp blueberries (fresh, cut in half, or mashed)
- 1 tbsp grated apple
- 1 tbsp unsweetened peanut or almond butter (if no nut allergy)

## Instructions

1. **Blend oats:** Add rolled oats to a blender or food processor. Blend for 30–45 seconds until you have a fine oat flour. This is the key step — fine oat flour makes smooth, soft pancakes rather than gritty ones.
2. **Mash bananas:** Mash the bananas with a fork until completely smooth in a large bowl.
3. **Combine:** Add eggs, milk, baking powder, cinnamon, and vanilla to the mashed banana. Mix well. Add the blended oat flour and stir until a smooth, thick batter forms. Let rest 3–4 minutes — the oats absorb liquid and the batter thickens.
4. **Adjust consistency:** The batter should drop slowly from a spoon. If too thick, add 1 tbsp milk. If too thin, add 1 tbsp oat flour.
5. **Heat pan:** Place a non-stick skillet over low-medium heat. Add a small amount of coconut oil or butter.
6. **Cook small:** Drop 1 tbsp of batter per pancake (about 6 cm circles). If adding blueberries, press 2–3 halves onto each pancake now. Cook 2–3 minutes until bubbles appear on the surface and edges look set.
7. **Flip:** Turn carefully and cook 1–2 minutes more until lightly golden.
8. **Cool completely** before serving. Cut into strips or small pieces appropriate for your toddler's pincer grip.

## Chef's Tips

- The riper (and more spotted) the banana, the sweeter and more flavourful — perfectly ripe bananas are essential here.
- Blending the oats to a fine flour makes all the difference in texture; coarse oats make lumpy pancakes that are harder for babies to manage.
- Make a big batch on the weekend and freeze in a single layer — reheat in a dry pan on low heat for 2 minutes per side.
- As your baby grows (18–24 months), you can fold in small bits of strawberry, grated pear, or a pinch of nutmeg for variety.
- These are denser and more filling than the banana-egg pancakes — good for a more substantial breakfast.
- **Using instant oats:** Blend only 10–15 seconds (they're already finer). Reduce milk to 60 ml and rest only 1–2 minutes — instant oats absorb liquid much faster. Add milk by the tablespoon if the batter gets too thick.
- **One large pancake:** Pour all the batter into a larger pan, lower the heat to minimum, and cover with a lid for 1–2 minutes so the centre cooks through before flipping. Cut into long strips — easier for little hands than small rounds.
- **Oil:** Use just half a teaspoon of coconut oil (best) or a small knob of unsalted butter. Spread it evenly with a paper towel — too much oil makes the pancake slide and cook unevenly.
