# Tamagoyaki

> Japan's iconic rolled omelette — lightly sweet, silky in texture, and endlessly versatile as a breakfast, bento staple, or sushi topping.

## Ingredients

- 8 large eggs
- 3 tbsp dashi stock (or water)
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp mirin
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp fine salt
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (sunflower or vegetable), for cooking

## Instructions

1. **Mix the egg base:** Crack eggs into a bowl. Add dashi, soy sauce, mirin, sugar, and salt. Beat gently with chopsticks or a fork — you want the whites and yolks combined but not frothy. Strain through a fine sieve for a silkier result.
2. **Heat the pan:** Heat a tamagoyaki pan or small non-stick skillet (20 cm / 8 in) over medium heat. Add a thin film of oil, wiping the excess with a folded paper towel.
3. **First layer:** Pour in about ¼ of the egg mixture and tilt the pan to coat. When the surface is just set but still slightly wet (about 1 minute), roll it up from one end into a log using chopsticks or a spatula.
4. **Repeat:** Push the rolled egg to one end. Oil the empty pan again. Pour in another ¼ of the mixture, lifting the existing roll so new egg flows underneath. Roll up again around the existing log.
5. **Continue** with remaining egg in 2 more layers, always rolling toward the same end.
6. **Shape:** Remove from heat. Place on a bamboo mat or cling film and roll tightly. Leave for 3 minutes, then slice into rounds approximately 2 cm thick.

## Chef's Tips

- A proper rectangular tamagoyaki pan (makiyakinabe) makes this much easier and produces a neater shape.
- The lower the heat the more even the colour — patience pays off.
- Add 1 tbsp of finely chopped spring onion or ½ tsp of grated ginger to the egg mix for a savoury version.
- For sushi-grade tamagoyaki, omit sugar and add a touch more salt and soy sauce.
